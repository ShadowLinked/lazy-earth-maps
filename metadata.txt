about=Focused on Google Maps, Street View, and Google Earth.
	Google Maps: roadmap / satellite / terrain, Street View at centre or any clicked point, drop a pin, copy link. Zoom is latitude-corrected, and the view fits your selected features when there is a selection.
	Street View Coverage: Toggle a live, free visual overlay showing real Google Street View coverage (blue lines) directly in your QGIS canvas!
	Google Earth: fly to the current view (rotation + tilt aware) and send the active layer, the selected features, or all visible vector layers as styled KMZ/KML with attribute pop-ups. Opens straight in Google Earth Pro if installed; otherwise a guided Earth Web hand-off.
	Settings: Accessible directly from Plugins > Lazy Earth & Maps > Settings... and Settings > Options > Lazy Earth & Maps.
	Derived from "Open Google Maps" by Harry King (GPL). See NOTICE.txt.
